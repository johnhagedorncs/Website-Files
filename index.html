About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at UC Santa Barbara. This summer I was an AI/ML intern at BrightAI, building anomaly detection for industrial gas compressors: PyTorch models trained on high-frequency vibration data, pipelines that pull that data out of S3, and the packaging and Kubernetes workflows that run it in production.

What I care about most is whether a result is real. My recent projects are about the cases where the standard approach reports something that isn't there, and what to do about it. Before that I built full-stack web apps and monitoring tools, a few of which are below.
